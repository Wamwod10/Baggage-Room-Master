Usage Rules

RULES FOR USING THE STORAGE ROOM!

Strictly follow the instructions for using the automated storage room!

Pay only for the first 1 (first) hour of storage use – 20,000 soums, then the system will automatically calculate the time and amount of storage use and request the remaining amount to release the luggage.

Enter the amount strictly according to the rate (20,000 soums of any denomination) ATTENTION!!! - THE EQUIPMENT DOES NOT GIVE CHANGE!

Only 1 (one) piece of luggage is placed in each cell and paid for separately!

Be sure to remember (write down) the phone number and password when registering luggage!

For each paid cell, a different phone number is entered; the same phone number cannot be registered for multiple cells!

Keep in mind that the storage room is automated, and in case of errors when retrieving luggage due to your fault, calling an employee will take 30 (thirty) minutes or more.

Also, a reminder: the administration is not responsible for improper and illiterate use of the storage room, and is not liable for the consequences of damage in such cases.

Kindly request: do not place your luggage in an already open cell that you did not pay for!
